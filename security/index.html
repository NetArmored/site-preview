How this site handles your information

  • The AI Opportunity Scan shows results in your browser. Answers are emailed to you and to NetArmored only if you agree; otherwise they stay in the tab. The ROI Explorer runs in your browser and is not transmitted.
  • The live site will store only lead information reasonably necessary to respond, with protected administrative access, defined retention and export capability.
  • Visitor-submitted business information is never used to train AI models without explicit authorization and a valid processing arrangement.
  • Information is not sent to third-party AI services without your awareness and consent.
  • Analytics, if added, will be configured not to expose personally identifying information.
  • The site uses security headers, no third-party scripts, and self-hosted fonts.
