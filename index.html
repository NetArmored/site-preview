Responsible AI, security & trust

Business transformation with security engineered into every step.

Here is how NetArmored approaches it — described plainly, without guarantees we can’t back up.

Data privacy

Collect only what’s needed; classify data before it touches any AI.

Access control

Least-privilege, role-based access with multi-factor authentication.

Encryption

In transit and at rest, using current standards.

Secure integrations

Scoped credentials, stored securely, rotated and logged.

AI governance

Approved tools, clear usage policy, periodic review.

Human approval

People approve consequential actions: payments, commitments, public posts.

Private & local AI options

Sensitive workloads can stay inside infrastructure you control.

Vendor evaluation

Data-handling terms reviewed before any provider is used.

Auditability

Activity logs so decisions and actions can be reviewed.

Operational resilience

Backups, fallbacks and recovery plans when a service is unavailable.

These are NetArmored’s recommended engineering practices. They are not independently certified controls or compliance attestations. Where certification or regulation applies, NetArmored will say so explicitly and document the basis.
