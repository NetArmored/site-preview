Privacy Notice

Effective November 25, 2026

This notice explains how NetArmored Consulting LLC ("NetArmored," "we") handles information collected through NetArmored.com. Questions or requests: hello@netarmored.com. Current clients may also use support@netarmored.com.

What we collect

  • When you send the contact form: business name, your name, email, optional phone, industry, the challenges and services you select or describe, whether you agreed to be contacted, whether you opted in to occasional insights, the page you came from, and the date and time.
  • Automatically with a form submission: your IP address and your browser's user-agent string. We use these to prevent spam and abuse.
  • When you browse: our website host keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) to deliver and secure the site.
  • The ROI Explorer and the AI Advisor demo run in your browser. What you enter in them is not sent to us or stored, unless you choose to include it in the contact form.
  • The AI Opportunity Scan shows your results on screen either way. If you skip or decline the email step, your answers stay in that browser tab and are not sent. If you agree, we email your name, email, optional phone, optional business name, industry, and your answers — including the items you star as most important — to you and to NetArmored at hello@netarmored.com. We use your IP address and browser type on that request to block spam.

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies or tracking. No third parties collect information about your online activity over time or across other websites through this site.

How we use it

To respond to your request and follow up about it, to protect the site from spam and abuse, and to keep business records. If you agree at the end of the AI Opportunity Scan, we email that report to you and use it to follow up about your business. We send occasional insights only if you opt in. Every insights email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who receives it

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and our email: our website host (Cloudflare), the email delivery service that sends form submissions and scan reports (Resend), and our email provider (DreamHost). If you agree to the scan email, Cloudflare Workers AI may draft the summary. That service receives the scan answers, scores, and industry only — not your name, email, phone, or business name. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business. We don't sell your information, and we don't share it for others' advertising.

AI and your information

We don't use the information you send us to train AI models. The on-screen scan is a set of rules in your browser, not an AI service. If you agree to email the report, an AI service (Cloudflare Workers AI) may draft the summary from your answers, scores, and industry only. It does not receive your name, email, phone, or business name. If it is unavailable, we send a standard summary instead. We don't send your information to other AI services without your knowledge and consent.

Please don't send sensitive information through the form

Don't include passwords, access credentials, payment details, health information, or details of an active security incident. Call (702) 714-0714 and we'll arrange a secure way to share them.

Retention

We keep inquiry information for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or we must keep it longer for legal or accounting reasons. Technical logs are kept for a shorter period by our hosting provider.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards. No website or email system is perfectly secure.

Your choices

Email hello@netarmored.com to review, correct or delete your information, or to stop receiving insights. You can also unsubscribe from any insights email. Nevada residents may send a request about the sale of covered information to the same address. We do not sell covered information.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this notice, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If the changes are significant, we'll also put a notice on the home page.
